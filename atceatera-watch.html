Atceatera Watch

Atceatera Watch is a short-form video commercial project created to celebrate the anniversary of a Melbourne-based vintage watch business. Developed in an interview format, the piece explores the founder's journey, inspirations, values, and the story behind the brand she has built. As one of my first projects working with a camera and approaching video production through a more cinematic lens, it provided an opportunity to experiment with visual storytelling, interview-based content, and social media-focused editing. The final video was designed to capture the personality of the business while highlighting an important milestone in its growth.
