Georgian Nature

"Georgian Nature" is a collection of photographs taken while travelling through Georgia. Drawn to the country's striking landscapes, I used photography as a way of documenting moments that felt both vast and intimate — from expansive mountain views to quieter details found along the journey. Rather than focusing on specific locations, the project captures my experience of moving through these environments and the sense of calm, scale, and connection to nature they inspired.
