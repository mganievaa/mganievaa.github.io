Open House Melbourne 2025

Open House Melbourne is an annual event that gives the public the opportunity to explore some of Melbourne’s significant buildings, spaces, and places, including locations that are not normally open to visitors. As part of the 2025 program, Russian House opened its doors to the public, with Young Guard presenting a live performance exploring the history of the building and its connection to the Russian community in Melbourne. I had the opportunity to document the event through photography and videography, capturing both the performance and the atmosphere surrounding it.
