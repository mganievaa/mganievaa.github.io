Short-Form Film Studies

A collection of self-directed video projects developed during a videography course, exploring the fundamentals of visual storytelling and short-form filmmaking. Working with a vertical, social-media-focused format, I experimented with different approaches to montage, including linear and more transition-driven editing, while applying principles of composition, lighting, framing, and pacing. Each project gave me an opportunity to take the techniques introduced throughout the course and develop them independently through practical experimentation.
