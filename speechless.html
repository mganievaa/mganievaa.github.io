Speechless

"Speechless" is a photography series exploring the quiet presence of lived-in spaces after their stories have faded. Focusing on empty interiors, worn surfaces, and repetitive architectural details, the images capture moments suspended between occupation and abandonment. Rather than documenting a place, the project observes the atmosphere it holds—where silence becomes a form of communication, and traces of everyday life suggest memories that remain long after words have disappeared.
